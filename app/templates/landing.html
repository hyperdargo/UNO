The cards beyond the classic deck

Everything you know is still here. These come with the other two modes.

What No Mercy adds

  • {{ card("green", "skip_all", "s") }}

    Skip Everyone

    Every other player is skipped. You go again.

  • {{ card("yellow", "discard_all", "s") }}

    Discard All

    Play it and throw out every other card of that color with it.

  • {{ card("blue", "draw4", "s") }}

    Draw 4

    A colored +4. Matches like any colored card and stacks.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_reverse_draw4", "s", label="wild reverse draw 4") }}

    Wild Reverse Draw 4

    Flips the direction, then the new next player faces +4.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_draw6", "s") }}

    Wild Draw 6

    Pick a color. Next player stacks or draws 6.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_draw10", "s") }}

    Wild Draw 10

    The biggest draw in the deck. Nothing smaller can answer it.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_roulette", "s") }}

    Color Roulette

    The next player names a color and flips cards until it shows up. They keep every card flipped.

What Flip adds

  • {{ card("blue", "flip", "s") }}

    Flip

    Turns the deck, both piles and every hand over. The dark side stays in play until someone flips it back.

  • {{ card("red", "draw1", "s") }}

    Draw One

    The light side plays gently: the next player takes one card and misses their turn.

  • {{ card("orange", "draw5", "s") }}

    Draw Five

    The same idea on the dark side. Five cards, no turn.

  • {{ card("purple", "skip_all", "s") }}

    Skip Everyone

    Every other player loses their turn and it comes straight back to you.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_draw_color", "s", label="wild draw color") }}

    Wild Draw Color

    Name a color. The next player draws until they find it, however long that takes.

  • {{ card("teal", "7", "s") }}

    Pink, teal, orange, purple

    The dark side has its own four colors. Same matching, meaner cards.

Getting a game going

  1. Open a table

    Name it, pick Normal, No Mercy or Flip, and set a 15, 30 or 60 second turn timer.

  2. Fill the seats

    Friends join from the lobby or with your private table code. Empty seats take bots. Up to 10 players.

  3. Deal

    Refresh or lose signal and your seat waits for you. If your timer runs out, a move is made so the table keeps going.
