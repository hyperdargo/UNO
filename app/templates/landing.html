Two ways to play

Pick the mode when you open a table. Everyone at the table plays the same rules.

Rule Normal No Mercy
Deck108 cards168 cards

The cards only No Mercy has

Everything from the classic deck is still here. These are added on top.

  • {{ card("green", "skip_all", "s") }}

    Skip Everyone

    Every other player is skipped. You go again.

  • {{ card("yellow", "discard_all", "s") }}

    Discard All

    Play it and throw out every other card of that color with it.

  • {{ card("blue", "draw4", "s") }}

    Draw 4

    A colored +4. Matches like any colored card and stacks.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_reverse_draw4", "s", label="wild reverse draw 4") }}

    Wild Reverse Draw 4

    Flips the direction, then the new next player faces +4.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_draw6", "s") }}

    Wild Draw 6

    Pick a color. Next player stacks or draws 6.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_draw10", "s") }}

    Wild Draw 10

    The biggest draw in the deck. Nothing smaller can answer it.

  • {{ card("wild", "wild_roulette", "s") }}

    Color Roulette

    The next player names a color and flips cards until it shows up. They keep every card flipped.

Getting a game going

  1. Open a table

    Name it, choose Normal or No Mercy, and set a 15, 30 or 60 second turn timer.

  2. Fill the seats

    Friends join from the lobby or with your private table code. Empty seats take bots. Up to 10 players.

  3. Deal

    Refresh or lose signal and your seat waits for you. If your timer runs out, a move is made so the table keeps going.
